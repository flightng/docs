## Fly Your Way

**Three rate curves**, fully mixable:

- **Betaflight Rate** — The feel you know, seamless migration
- **Actual Rate** — Intuitive setup, what you set is what you get
- **FlightOne Rate** — Natural choice for FalcoX pilots

Yaw, Pitch, and Roll can each use different curves. Your flight style, defined by you.

## Complete Ecosystem

| Capability | Support |
|------------|---------|
| OSD Display | MSP DisplayPort protocol for digital VTX; some boards carry an onboard MAX7456 |
| Flight Recording | Onboard flash / SD-card Blackbox with deep analysis |
| RC Protocols | CRSF · SBUS · SBUS_FAST |
| Motor Protocol | Bidirectional DShot (150/300/600) |
